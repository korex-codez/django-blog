## 2026-11-11 - Eliminating N+1 Query Cascade on Post Listing Views
**Learning:** In Django template rendering of post cards (`home.html` and `post_list.html`), each post accesses `post.author.username`, `post.category.name`, `post.tags.all`, and `post.total_likes`. Without eager loading, rendering 4-6 posts triggers 4 queries per post (author, category, tags, and likes count), leading to an N+1 query cascade (e.g. 27 queries on home page). Furthermore, annotating `.annotate(like_count=Count('likes'))` clears the model's default `Meta.ordering`, requiring an explicit `.order_by('-created_at')` to maintain deterministic ordering and prevent pagination warnings.
**Action:** Always combine `.select_related('category', 'author')`, `.prefetch_related('tags')`, `.annotate(like_count=Count('likes'))`, and `.order_by('-created_at')` on `Post` listing querysets, and check `if hasattr(self, 'like_count')` in `Post.total_likes()`.
